The idea

One request. Hundreds of ways to answer it. Most of them wrong for you.

And the requests keep multiplying. Work is moving from a single chatbot to fleets of agents that call models on their own, so these choices now happen faster than any person, or any policy, can keep up with. Most teams still pick one model and hope it holds across everything they throw at it.

MIMIR turns each choice into a decision. It reads the request as it arrives, chooses the agent most likely to succeed on your terms, and keeps you inside the rules you have to follow. The hard calls happen in the layer, so they stop happening in your head.

One concrete moment

Imagine a Tuesday afternoon.

Someone in marketing pastes three CVs into a chatbot to summarize them before a meeting. They are not a lawyer. They do not know that, the moment those CVs are used to compare candidates, the request became a high risk task under EU law, or that the model they happened to open is not cleared to handle it.

MIMIR sees it in the request. It routes to an agent that is both capable and allowed, holds back the ones that are not, flags that the final hiring call needs a human, and records why. The work gets done. The line that should not be crossed is not crossed. Nobody had to know the rule by heart.

How it works

Three steps, every single request.

An agent is more than a model. It is a model together with where it runs, the prompt it is given, and the skills it can call. MIMIR reasons over all of it.

01
Predict

MIMIR reads fast, low latency signals from the incoming request and forecasts how well each available agent would handle it, with a sense of how sure it is. It scores each option before a single token runs, generalizing from how past requests turned out to ones it has never seen, not from vendor benchmarks.

02
Comply

In the same pass, it reads what the request is really about and removes every agent you are not permitted to use, by law, policy or risk. Nothing forbidden ever reaches a model.

03
Route

From the agents that remain, MIMIR picks the one with the best predicted outcome for your priorities. A quick summary does not need a frontier model; a hard reasoning task is worth one. You stop overpaying for easy work, get the reason for every choice, and can override it whenever you want.

Our edge

Routing that goes deeper, and sees what others miss.

Soft agent characteristics

We route on more than a model name.

Most routers stop at "which model." MIMIR predicts outcomes across the whole agent: model, hosting, prompt and skills. The same model with a sharper prompt or the right tool is a different agent to us, and often the better answer.

Compliance, made interesting

A decision read from the request itself.

Others ask only where your data is stored. MIMIR reads what the request is actually about and decides which agents are even allowed to touch it, using similarity to structured, in depth analysis of real cases.

Every verdict is interpretable: we can show you the exact case that drove it, and every decision lands in an audit trail you can stand behind. It is a routing decision and a compliance decision in a single pass, and it is the part nobody else has.

Bring your own

New signals and agents drop straight in.

Have your own endpoints, your own policies, a risk nobody else screens for? Adding a signal or an agent to MIMIR is routine, so the layer fits the rules you actually operate under, not a generic checklist.

Why it compounds

Every routed request sharpens the next.

MIMIR sits in the one place that sees the request, the choice and the outcome together. That position is a data flywheel: every routed request becomes training signal, so the predictor keeps improving through continual, life-long learning. The more it routes, the better it gets, for everyone behind the layer.

Above the stack
